FAQ

What buyers
ask me most.

The developer

SiSol Soluciones Inmobiliarias has more than 25 years in Mexican real estate: it designs, manages, builds and sells vacation, residential and investment developments, with 30+ completed projects in 10 states. Punta Pacífico is backed by INVEX.

When are the homes delivered?

Estimated delivery is mid-2028. The 20 monthly payments run during construction.

Can I pay the 80% with a mortgage?

Yes. The 80% at delivery can be covered with a mortgage, your own funds or a combination. I’ll help you review the best option.

What’s included at delivery?

Kitchen with granite bar, stove, oven and range hood, closets, mini-split A/C, tempered-glass showers, digital lock, lights and climate from the app, and a roof with half bath, bar with sink, grill and pergola.

Can foreigners buy here?

Yes. Foreign buyers commonly purchase in Baja California through a bank trust (fideicomiso). I’ll walk you through the process and documents.

Does it work as a vacation rental?

Ensenada draws steady tourism for its wine, food and proximity to California. Ask me for the ROI analysis and we’ll review the numbers.

Where is the sales office?

In northern Ensenada. Open it in Google Maps and book an appointment so I can meet you in person.

How much do I need to reserve?

Message me on WhatsApp and I’ll share the reservation amount and the units available this week.
